About Uncle Bubba's Drones

Buying drone parts means hopping between a half-dozen sites. A motor is cheapest on one, the matching ESC is only in stock on another, and the OEM replacement only exists on the manufacturer's own store. You end up with multiple different tabs open and multiple hours of your time wasted.

Our goal is to simplify this process. Search once, see what a part costs across different retailers, and click through to buy from whichever one suits you.

What we do

  • Index real drone parts from real retailers — motors, ESCs, frames, propellers, batteries, cameras, and so much more
  • Show prices side by side so you can see the actual difference between retailers
  • List manufacturer-stated compatibility so you know what a part fits before you buy
  • Link straight to the retailer's own product page — no middleman, no markup

What we don't do

  • We don't sell anything ourselves, hold inventory, or process payments
  • We don't rank listings by what pays us most — cheapest is listed first
  • We don't invent specs or compatibility. If we can't verify something, we note that rather than make up information

Who runs this

Me, a vegetation management pilot who is tired of spending hours trying to find a part that will take less than 5 minutes to install. A pilot who is tired of losing a week's worth of work because the only part I could find to solve my problem is being shipped from overseas. So I made this website to help myself and others who may also be in my position.
